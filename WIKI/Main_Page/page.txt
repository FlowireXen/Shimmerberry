Shimmerberry is a mod by Flowire with art by unkokat that expands the game by adding vanilla-like and custom content.<br>
It adds several new [[/Jokers/|Jokers]] and [[/Challenges/|Challenges]], a couple of [[/Boss Blinds/|Boss Blinds]], [[/Decks/|Decks]] and [[/Tags/|Tags]], and various other content.
