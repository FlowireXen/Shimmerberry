<big>When starting the Challenge:</big>
* Applies {{ct|money|Gold Stake}} difficulty
* Secretly [[Shimmerberry/Tags|{{ct|purple|Dooms}}]] the Player, reveals {{ct|purple|Doom}} after beating {{ct|orange|Ante 1}}
* Hides stats for all areas {{ct|grey|(instead of showing "0/5 Jokers", it shows either "NaN" or "???")}}
* Increases {{ct|orange|Joker}} and {{ct|orange|Consumable}} slots by {{ct|orange|1}}
* Sets {{ct|orange|Hand Size}} to a random number between {{ct|orange|6 and 12}}
* {{ct|codegreen|Randomizes}} the entire Deck
* Permanently reduces {{ct|money|Shop}} prices by {{ct|money|20%}}
* Permanently reduces {{ct|green|Reroll}} cost by {{ct|money|$2}}
* Permanently adds {{ct|orange|1}} {{vj|Booster_Packs|Booster-Pack|img=Standard_Pack_1_(Vanilla).png|nott=true}} to all {{ct|money|Shops}}
* Permanently {{ct|red|increases}} all {{ct|orange|Blind Sizes}} by {{ct|darkpurple|25%}}
* Sets the Winning Ante to {{ct|orange|8}} {{ct|grey|(the Winning Ante stays hidden)}}
* Sets up a custom Game Over; if the player fails, it will always show "{{ct|pink|ERROR}}"
----
<big>While playing:</big>
* When skipping a {{ct|orange|Blind}}, {{ct|green|1 in 2}} chance to gain a {{vt|Double Tag}} {{ct|grey|(doesn't duplicate the Tag obtained by skipping)}}
* When {{ct|green|Rerolling}} the {{ct|money|Shop}}, it will always generate a new {{vj|Booster_Packs|Booster-Pack|img=Standard_Pack_1_(Vanilla).png|nott=true}}
* When using a {{ct|orange|Consumable}}, {{ct|green|1 in 3}} chance to gain a copy
* When '''Obtaining''' a {{ct|orange|Joker}}, {{ct|green|1 in 4}} chance to  {{ct|green|gain}} a {{ct|orange|Joker Slot}}
* When '''Selling''' a {{ct|orange|Joker}}, {{ct|green|1 in 4}} chance to {{ct|red|lose}} a {{ct|orange|Joker Slot}}
* '''Upping the Ante''' has a {{ct|green|1 in 4}} chance to fail
* When a {{ct|orange|Boss Blind}} is defeated, reduce all {{ct|money|Shop}} prices by {{ct|money|5%}} and {{ct|red|increases}} all {{ct|orange|Blind Sizes}} by {{ct|darkpurple|25%}}
* When playing a {{ct|blue|Hand}} while having more than {{ct|orange|0}} {{ct|red|Discards}}, temporarily increase the {{ct|orange|Hand Size}} by {{ct|orange|3}}
